Capy Takes uploader - Privacy Policy

Capy Takes uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Capy Takes YouTube channel (capytakes@gmail.com) to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API, read the channel's own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API, and save those statistics to the owner's Google Drive.

It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel and, on Google Drive, only the files it creates itself, with the owner's permission. It does not collect, store, sell or share data from any other person. The access tokens stay on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: capytakes@gmail.com
